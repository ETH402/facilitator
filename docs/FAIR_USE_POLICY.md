# Fair use policy

ETH402 is open infrastructure with finite operational capacity. Users must not
attempt denial of service, gas draining, replay, credential abuse, unauthorized
access, metrics scraping that impairs service, or illegal activity.

Operators may rate-limit, require manual review, suspend integrations, or
reject traffic to protect the service. Controls must not mutate standard x402
payloads or create custody. This policy is not a claim that ETH402 verifies a
business's legitimacy and is not a commercial pricing plan.
